Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

What do I need to use VoiceCart?
VoiceCart is iPhone only. You need an iPhone running iOS 16 or later with the Shortcuts app (it comes pre-installed). You'll also need the Instacart app installed on your phone. Unfortunately, VoiceCart does not work on Android devices or iPads.

Is this a subscription?
No! VoiceCart is a one-time purchase. Pay once and use it forever. No monthly fees, no annual renewals, no hidden charges.

How does the shortcut work?
When you activate the shortcut (via Home Screen icon or Action Button), it prompts you to speak. Your voice is converted to text and used to search your selected store on Instacart. The search results page opens automatically in the Instacart app.

Can I use multiple stores?
Yes! Each store has its own shortcut. With the Unlimited plan ($10), you get shortcuts for all supported stores. With the Single Store plan ($3), you choose one store. You can always upgrade later.

What's the Action Button?
The Action Button is a customizable button on iPhone 15 Pro and newer models. You can set it to run any shortcut with a single press. Assign VoiceCart to it for instant voice searches without even unlocking your phone or opening an app.

How do I get the shortcut after purchase?
After your purchase is confirmed, you'll receive an email with download links for your shortcut(s). Simply tap the link on your iPhone to add it to your Shortcuts app. The whole process takes less than a minute.

Does this add items to my cart automatically?
VoiceCart searches for items and opens the results in Instacart. From there, you can tap to add specific items to your cart. This gives you control over exactly which product, brand, and size you want.
